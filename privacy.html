Privacy Policy – Move To The Back!

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy describes how Move To The Back! ("the game"), developed by Agorga Games, handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no advertising, and it does not use analytics or tracking services.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your level progress, stars and settings (sound, music, vibration, language) only on your own device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Permissions

The game only uses vibration for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the settings. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the game ever adds features that collect data (for example ads or analytics), this policy will be updated before those features are released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions: info@agorgagames.com
